About Ciji and creating a place where beauty meets magic.

Ciji is the heart and soul of Enchanted Sanctuary. A compassionate healer, intuitive guide, and empowering coach.

Her life’s journey, woven with resilience, self-discovery, and healing, has shaped her into a beacon of wisdom, strength, and understanding for those seeking transformation

Ciji’s path began in beauty therapy, driven by a desire to help women feel their best, take time for themselves, and experience relaxation and rejuvenation. Over time, life’s challenges, including leaving an abusive relationship, navigating the grief of losing a loved one, and parenting a neurodiverse child, opened her eyes to a deeper purpose.

Through her own healing journey, Ciji discovered the profound benefits of Reiki and other spiritual and therapeutic practices. These modalities not only transformed her life but revealed her ability to guide others on their paths to healing and empowerment. She realised that true well-being extends beyond the physical, encompassing emotional, social, and spiritual harmony.

Today, Ciji blends her deep commitment to self-care and beauty with her wealth of knowledge in energy healing, mindfulness, and coaching. Her work seamlessly integrates her beauty therapy expertise with transformative holistic practices, creating a well-rounded approach that nurtures both the outer and inner self.

A Philosophy of Empowerment

Ciji believes healing is not only a journey but a ripple effect. When we heal ourselves, we inspire positive change in our families, communities, and the world. Her mission is to help individuals reconnect with their inner magic, reclaim their self-worth, and align with their highest selves.

“Everything is energy, and energy is everything. When we align our energy, we transform not only ourselves but the world around us.”

A Journey of Renewal

Ciji’s integrative approach creates a sanctuary for every part of you, mind, body, and soul, to heal, thrive, and shine. Whether you seek beauty therapy to feel refreshed and confident or holistic healing to embrace transformation, her sanctuary offers a safe, nurturing space to explore your strengths, rewrite your story, and embrace the joy, confidence, and connection you deserve.

With Ciji as your guide, Enchanted Sanctuary is more than a place of healing; it is a haven of empowerment, growth, and transformation. Step into this sacred space, rediscover your magic, and create a ripple of healing that transforms your world.
