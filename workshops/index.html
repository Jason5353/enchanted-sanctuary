Phoenix Freedom Workshop

The Phoenix Freedom Workshop Series is a transformative programme created specifically for survivors of domestic abuse, offering a safe and nurturing space to heal, reclaim power, and rebuild self-worth. As a certified domestic abuse coach and empathetic healer, Ciji uses a multi-dimensional therapeutic approach to guide participants through their healing journey. This workshop blends spiritual healing, coaching, and group support to help you release the trauma of your past, rediscover your inner strength, and step into the life you truly deserve.
Through energy healing, mindfulness techniques, and empowering coaching, you'll let go of limiting beliefs and embrace your personal power. The Phoenix Freedom Workshop offers a supportive community of women who understand your journey, allowing you to heal and grow together. By the end of this workshop, you’ll feel empowered, confident, and ready to rise from the ashes of your past and step boldly into your future.

Parent and Child Emotional Regulation Workshop

Group workshops - family £600 | Single parent discounted price £500 | Individual 1 :1 Tailored workshop £900.
Designed for parents of highly sensitive or neurodiverse children, this interactive workshop series focuses on emotional regulation, communication, and building stronger family connections. Through a series of four sessions, you’ll learn valuable techniques to help your child manage their emotions, reduce anxiety, and develop self-awareness.
The workshops provide a compassionate and supportive environment where both parents and children can come together to explore emotional regulation tools and understand each other's needs. With Ciji’s guidance, you’ll gain insight into how to best support your sensitive child while strengthening your bond.
Whether you’re navigating the challenges of parenting a neurodiverse child or simply seeking more effective ways to communicate, this workshop empowers both parents and children with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to thrive.
