The Enchanted Healing Circle.

Book your session...

The Enchanted Healing Circle is a sacred space designed to empower, heal, and uplift women on their journey to self-discovery.

£25.00 | Monthly | 6pm-8:30pm at Barnwood Reading Rooms, Gloucester. Also virtualy online.

Join a transformative and empowering evening circle where women gather to connect, heal, and support each other.

Through guided meditations, Reiki healing, energy work, and spiritual rituals, you'll experience a safe and sacred space to release what no longer serves you and embrace your divine feminine energy.

Rooted in the age-old tradition of women’s circles, this healing journey is about collective growth and empowerment. Whether you speak your truth or simply listen, you’ll leave feeling uplifted, renewed, and connected to the magic within.

The circle falls monthly on the weekend closest to the full moon. Tickets are available on Eventbrite.
